# XP Tank

A block that automatically stores the XP generated by every [AFK Farm](/en/farms/) in the **same chunk** — no need to touch each farm individually, just place it anywhere in the chunk.

## Capacity

Holds up to **level 30** worth of XP (1,395 points). Once full, extra XP from your farms is lost until you collect it — same as an overflowing chest.

## Visual

A floating display above the tank shows your current **Level** and a progress bar to the next one, and the glass fills up with a rising green liquid as XP accumulates. Only shows up while you're aiming at the tank from within 8 blocks.

## How to collect

Three ways to use it, based on what you're holding when you right-click:

| You're holding | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Empty hand** | Absorb all the stored XP as levels, instantly. |
| **Glass Bottle** | Consumes 1 bottle + 7 xp from the tank, hands you a Bottle o' Enchanting. |
| **Damaged item with Mending** | Instantly repairs the item's durability using the tank's XP (same 2-durability-per-XP rate as vanilla Mending). |

## Recipe

4 Iron Ingots (corners) + 4 Glass (edges) + 1 Bottle o' Enchanting (center).

```
I G I
G B G
I G I
```

`I` = Iron Ingot · `G` = Glass · `B` = Bottle o' Enchanting
