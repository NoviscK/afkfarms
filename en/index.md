# AFK Farms

A Minecraft mod (Fabric 1.21.11) that runs mob farms automatically — even while you're offline.

## How it works

1. Craft an **AFK Farm** (Overworld mobs) or an **AFK Nether Farm** (Nether/End/Trial Chambers mobs).
2. Put the right activation item in the top slot — every mob has its own (see the [full farm list](/en/farms/)).
3. Feed it fuel: Coal, Charcoal, Lava Bucket, or the advanced fuels (Blaze Powder, Nether Star).
4. Connect a Hopper + Chest below or beside the block.
5. Leave it running. It even works offline — when you come back, the accumulated drops are calculated and delivered all at once.

## Main features

- **32 different farms**, from the Overworld to the Nether, with drops matching vanilla's real loot tables.
- **[XP Tank](/en/xp-tank/)** — automatically stores the XP from every farm in the chunk.
- **[Soul Bottle](/en/soul-bottle/)** — for the most valuable/dangerous mobs, you need to capture their soul by really killing them first.
- **Durability** — activation items wear out over time and break, encouraging you to keep restocking.
- **[`config.json`](/en/config/)** — tune drops, XP, fuel, and durability without touching any code.
