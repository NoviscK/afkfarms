# Farm List

Every farm works the same way: put the **activation item** in the top slot of the right block, feed it fuel, and connect a Hopper + Chest below or beside it. Drops below match vanilla's real loot tables (pulled directly from the game's data).

> 💡 Exact percentages and counts can be tuned in [`config.json`](/en/config/).

## 🌾 AFK Farm — Plains

| Mob | Activation Item | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Cow | Leather | Leather (0–2), Raw Beef (1–3) |
| Sheep | White Wool | Mutton (1–2), White Wool (1) |
| Chicken | Feather | Feather (0–2), Raw Chicken (1) |
| Pig | Pork Chop | Raw Porkchop (1–3) |
| Rabbit | Rabbit Foot | Raw Rabbit (1), Rabbit Hide (0–1), Rabbit Foot (10%) |
| Bee | Honeycomb | Honeycomb (35%)¹ |

¹ *Bees don't drop anything in vanilla — a small exception to keep this farm useful.*
