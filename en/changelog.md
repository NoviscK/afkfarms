# Changelog

## AFK Farms — v1.2.0

### New Features

- **XP Tank** — new block that automatically stores the XP generated by every AFK Farm in the same chunk. No need to attach it to each individual farm — just place it anywhere in the chunk and it collects from all of them.
- Holds up to **level 30** worth of XP. Once full, extra XP from your farms is lost until you collect it (same as an overflowing chest).
- Floating display above the tank shows your current **Level** and a progress bar to the next one, plus the glass fills up with a rising green liquid as XP accumulates. Only shows up while you're aiming at the tank from within 8 blocks, to keep it out of the way otherwise.
- Three ways to use it, based on what you're holding when you right-click:
  - **Empty hand** — absorb all the stored XP as levels, instantly.
  - **Glass Bottle** — consumes 1 bottle + XP from the tank to hand you a Bottle o' Enchanting.
  - **Damaged item with Mending** — instantly repairs the item's durability using the tank's XP (same 2-durability-per-XP rate as vanilla Mending).
- **XP Tank recipe** — 4 Iron Ingots (corners) + 4 Glass (edges) + 1 Bottle o' Enchanting (center).

### Farm Overhaul

- All farm drops (item chances, min/max counts) were rebuilt to match **vanilla's real loot tables** as closely as possible, pulled directly from the game's own data. Several farms are now more accurate than before (Witch now correctly drops 4-8 Redstone; Iron Golem always drops both Iron Ingots and Poppies; Blaze no longer drops Blaze Powder, since vanilla Blazes never do).
- **Activation items are no longer permanent.** Every farm's activation item (Leather, Bone, Ender Pearl, etc.) now has a limited number of uses (64 by default) and breaks once exhausted — you'll need to restock it, just like fuel. This can be turned off in the config.
- **2 new farms**: Breeze (Wind Charge, on the regular AFK Farm) and Wither Skeleton (AFK Nether Farm — powered by the new Soul Bottle, not a plain item).
- Fuel is now configurable per-type: Coal/Charcoal (60s/drop), Lava Bucket (30s/drop), and two new higher-tier fuels for the Nether farm's toughest mobs — Blaze Powder (15s/drop) and Nether Star (5s/drop).
- **New `config.json`** lets you tune everything above without touching code: drop chances, XP per drop, fuel timings, durability, and which mobs exist at all.

### Soul Bottle

- For the Nether's most dangerous mobs (starting with Wither Skeleton), a plain activation item isn't enough. Craft an empty **Soul Bottle** (Glass Bottle + Soul Sand), carry it in your inventory, and land the killing blow on the mob for real — it becomes a filled Soul Bottle for that specific mob, which then powers the farm instead of a plain item.
- Soul Bottles obey the same durability rule as every other activation item: each one is good for a limited number of drop cycles before it's spent.
- The bottle's model swaps automatically between its empty look and a glowing, essence-filled look once a soul is captured.

### Fixes

- Fixed Skeletons and Wither Skeletons in the AFK Farm terrarium swinging the wrong arm instead of properly aiming (Skeleton) or holding up their weapon (Wither Skeleton).
- The Farm Guide book now covers the XP Tank, Soul Bottle, and item durability, and lists Breeze/Wither Skeleton in the mob tables.
- Fixed the AFK Nether Farm accepting any farm's activation item (e.g. Leather spawning a Cow inside it). Each farm now only works in the block it's meant for.
