Changelog

AFK Farms — v1.3.0

New Blocks

  • AFK End City Farm — new block for Enderman and Shulker. Recipe: 4 Gold Ingots + 4 End Stone + 1 Ender Pearl.
  • AFK Aquatic Farm — new block for Squid, Glow Squid, Turtle, Guardian, Elder Guardian and Drowned. Recipe: 4 Gold Ingots + 4 Prismarine Shards + 1 Water Bucket. Mobs inside actually swim (tentacles, shell, swimming pose), with genuinely translucent water.

Facing

  • Every AFK Farm block now has a facing side, just like a furnace — it faces you when placed.

New Feature: Colored Wool Farming

  • The Sheep farm now accepts all 16 wool colors — whatever color goes in comes back out, and the terrarium sheep is dyed to match.

New Feature: Cooked Meat Chance

  • Cow, Sheep, Chicken, Pig, Rabbit and Hoglin have an 8% chance to drop their meat already cooked instead of raw.

Fixes

  • AFK Farm and AFK Nether Farm block models rebuilt from scratch with more detail.
  • Magma Cube now spawns in the right block (Nether) and hops with fire particles, instead of sliding slowly.
  • Zombified Piglin, Enderman, Shulker, Iron Golem and Husk got fixed animations (poses, teleport particles, no more sinking into the floor).
  • Every mob's walking pace now stays in sync with its own leg animation, matching real vanilla movement speed.
  • Fixed the Farm Guide's crafting recipe and the stretched Magma texture in the Nether block model.

Farm Reassignments

  • Enderman and Shulker → AFK End City Farm.
  • Squid, Glow Squid, Turtle, Guardian, Elder Guardian and Drowned → AFK Aquatic Farm.

AFK Farms — v1.2.0

New Features

  • XP Tank — automatically stores XP from every AFK Farm in the same chunk.
  • Holds up to level 30. Once full, extra XP is lost until collected.
  • Floating Level + progress bar display, glass fills with green liquid. Only shows aiming at the tank within 8 blocks.
  • Three ways to collect: empty hand (all), Glass Bottle (Bottle o' XP), damaged item w/ Mending (instant repair).
  • Recipe: 4 Iron Ingots + 4 Glass + 1 Bottle o' Enchanting.

Farm Overhaul

  • All drops rebuilt to match vanilla's real loot tables.
  • Activation items no longer last forever — 64 uses by default, then break.
  • 2 new farms: Breeze and Wither Skeleton (via Soul Bottle).
  • Fuel configurable per-type, including Blaze Powder and Nether Star.
  • New config.json to tune everything without touching code.

Soul Bottle

  • For the most dangerous mobs, craft an empty Soul Bottle and really kill the mob to capture it.
  • Follows the same durability rule as other activation items.
  • Model swaps automatically between empty and essence-filled.

Fixes

  • Skeleton/Wither Skeleton no longer swing the wrong arm — now properly aim the bow or raise the sword.
  • Farm Guide book updated with XP Tank, Soul Bottle, and durability.
  • AFK Nether Farm no longer accepts another farm's activation item.
