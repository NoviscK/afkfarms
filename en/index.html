Farms that work on their own.

AFK Farms automatically simulates mob deaths — Overworld, Nether, and even the most dangerous mobs. Leave it running, even offline, and come back to collect it all at once.

See all farms → Changelog

How it works

  1. Craft an AFK Farm (Overworld mobs) or an AFK Nether Farm.
  2. Put the right activation item in the top slot — every mob has its own (see the full list).
  3. Feed it fuel: Coal, Charcoal, Lava Bucket, or the advanced ones (Blaze Powder, Nether Star).
  4. Connect a Hopper + Chest below or beside the block.
  5. Leave it running. It works offline too — when you return, the accumulated drops are calculated and delivered at once.

Features

📋32 farms

From the Overworld to the Nether, with drops matching vanilla's real loot tables.

🧪XP Tank

Automatically stores XP from every farm in the chunk. No need to touch it.

🧴Soul Bottle

For the most valuable mobs, capture their soul by really killing them first.

⚙️config.json

Tune drops, xp, fuel and durability without touching any code.
